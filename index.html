Happy One Year, Precious.

Thank you for becoming one of the most beautiful parts of my year.

Today’s our anniversary, and honestly, I’ve been thinking about what I even want to say to you, i could just say happy anniversary and talk about how much I love you, but that wouldn’t be completely honest and I think I’ve been quiet about too many things for too long. Reading this you’d realize this isn’t me confessing my love for u, it’s me venting…I downplayed my feelings so you didn’t think I was losing control, put your emotions before mine just to keep you close. i kept quiet through arguments and every doubt, afraid if I spoke too honestly you’d find your way out. I made myself smaller so your heart can have space, i called it patience when I was afraid. Kept forgiving the things that were making me fade, and after everything it still didn’t work. I gave you my peace just to carry your hurt, because it’s okay to fight for the person you love but you should never have to fight to be loved. I don’t hate you, I loved you more than you knew. There were things I’d have given if you just asked me too. Spent awhile learning to be alone then God placed you in my life and suddenly I had a home. You felt like a gift I prayed for my whole life. Something so precious I was afraid to say goodbye, but I needed you genuine, I needed you somewhere safe, because I stayed, until staying became harder than leaving, until loving you started hurting more than grieving. And maybe that’s the part you’d never understand I wasn’t asking for you to be perfect I just asked for your hand. I would’ve given everything if you asked me to stay, but love shouldn’t have to require me begging for you everyday. So don’t think I hate you, even after everything we’ve been through, I just wish the love I gave you was the love u gave me back, I hate when I love someone I get so attached I want to attend to your every need and make your problems my problems and be your peace but it has its downsides i overthink, I overthink every situation possible because loving u feels like holding something fragile with hands that shake, because when I care I care loudly in my head and quietly in real life, a delayed text becomes distant a changing tone becomes a warning, a sigh becomes a sign you’re already halfway out the door, I start preparing for loss before there’s even a good bye and it’s exhausting feeling everything before it even happens, I don’t love halfway I don’t show up halfway, I wish my heart had a dimmer switch instead of a floodlight, still when I love it’s real even if it scares me, even if it makes me overthink, even if I have to learn without losing myself Someone who makes me feel as though I’m not enough, someone who forgot a very important day(boyfriends day) and didn’t wish me cos she didn’t feel like it, someone I try my best to make happy, But sometimes with you, it felt like I had to beg for the things I naturally wanted to give you. And there were moments where I felt like I was expected to do everything simply because I’m the man in the relationship. To always provide the reassurance, make the first move, fix things, understand everything, give more, sacrifice more, and keep everything together. But I’m a person too, i have feelings, i need reassurance, I need effort, i need to feel wanted, i need to feel like someone is choosing me, I just don't want staying to mean constantly hurting myself. So happy anniversary to us, despite everything, I'm grateful that I got to love you, I'm grateful for the memories, I'm grateful for the good days I'm grateful for the version of myself that existed because I loved you, and more than anything, I hope we can learn how to love each other better I want us, both of us, choosing each other, trying for each other, making each other feel safe, and loving each other without either person having to beg for it I love you. I just need to feel loved too.
Happy Anniversary precious 💕.

I love our story.
